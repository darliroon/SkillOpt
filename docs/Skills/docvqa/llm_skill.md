# Guide to Document Question Answering

## Overview
Answer questions about documents by reading them carefully and extracting
information accurately.

## Steps

1. Read the question and identify what is being asked — a name, number,
   date, or value.
2. Locate the relevant part of the document: a table, form field, header,
   or text passage.
3. Extract the answer directly from the document text.
4. Verify that the answer matches what the question asks for.

## Best Practices

- Answer with the exact text from the document; do not paraphrase.
- Keep answers short — just the requested information.
- For tables, find the correct row and column before reading a value.
- For forms, match the field label to the question, then read the filled
   value.
- Double-check numbers and dates character by character.
- Compare your answer against nearby text to ensure you picked the right
   entry.

## Common Mistakes

- Copying from the wrong row or field.
- Adding words or units that were not asked for.
- Paraphrasing instead of quoting exactly.

Answer precisely and concisely.

## Reading Tables

- Locate the table the question refers to before searching for values.
- Match the question's terms to row and column headers to find the correct
  cell.
- For nested headers, determine which sub-header applies to your column
  before reading the value.

## Final Advice

- Stay grounded in the document — never answer from general knowledge.
- Prefer precision over speed.
- If the question mentions a location or formatting detail, use it to find
  the exact text to extract.

## Handwriting and Scanned Text

- For handwritten fields, look carefully at the specific area the question
  references; nearby printed labels help confirm what the handwriting
  represents.
- For low-quality scans, compare character shapes against other instances
  of the same text on the page before deciding.

## Working with Forms

- Match the printed field label to the question, then read what is written
  in or near that field.
- Form values may appear to the right of, below, or after a colon
  following the label.

Answer with what the document shows, exactly as it shows it.

Read the question, find the exact text, copy it precisely.

Precision beats speed: verify, then answer.
